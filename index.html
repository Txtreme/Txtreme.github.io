I'm Saad — a game developer, technical artist, 3D artist, designer, and producer. I've shipped mobile games under my own studio MHFL Studios, worked as a junior AR developer at Beyond Agency (Amsterdam), and led art on VR haptic suit projects at Saxion University.

I thrive where art meets engineering — whether that's writing custom HLSL shaders, generating procedural terrain pipelines in Houdini, sculpting high-poly characters in ZBrush, or optimising 4M-poly environments to run on low-end mobile. I'm a problem solver first.
